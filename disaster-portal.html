Glacial Lake Outburst (GLOF) & Severe Flash Flood Emergency Severe hydrological surge detected along the Trishuli, Bhote Koshi, and Langtang river corridors following sudden high-altitude glacial moraine breach and torrential 142mm cloudburst.
Mandatory Structural & Civilian Directives
Move immediately to higher ground (at least 30 meters above riverbed level) away from the Bhote Koshi, Trishuli, and Langtang river valleys.
Avoid all low-lying riverbanks, gravel quarry roads, riverside trails, and suspension footbridges subject to sudden hydraulic scouring.
Do not attempt vehicular crossings on flooded causeways or submerged culverts. Mudflows and debris torrents carry high kinetic force.
